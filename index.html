Ai Li (李爱)

Research Intern @ NUS NExT++ · Incoming Graduate Student @ ICT, CAS

Hi! I am Ai Li, currently a research intern at the NExT++ Research Center, National University of Singapore, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Yunshan Ma. I am pursuing my B.E. in Computational Advertising at the Communication University of China.

I will soon begin my graduate study at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT, CAS), advised by Prof. Liang Pang.

My research currently focuses on Latent Reasoning and Recommendation Systems. I am broadly interested in how models can reason in continuous latent spaces and how such reasoning can power more effective information access.

I'm always happy to chat and collaborate — feel free to reach out!
